La sebkha — passage à basse altitude

01_salt-flat · 3+3+2+2+2+3 = 15 s
Multi-shot air-to-air sequence, six cuts, fifteen seconds total. NO MUSIC.

SUBJECT — <Subject 1> is a twelve metre single-crew aircraft, pale warm grey hull, one circular nose intake that always leads.

STYLE — 1985 television documentary photography shot on 16mm, hard white midday light, heavy heat haze.

Shot one, three seconds. Very low over a dead flat white salt pan, <Subject 1> runs left to right and tears a low rooster tail of salt dust off the crust.
Shot two, three seconds. Tighter and level with <Subject 1>, heat shimmer eating its outline.

AUDIO — diegetic only: a hard flat thruster drone, wind roar across an open helicopter door.

Le canyon — mesas et gorges

02_canyon · 3+2+3+2+2+3 = 15 s
Multi-shot air-to-air sequence, six cuts, fifteen seconds total. NO MUSIC.

SUBJECT — <Subject 1> is a twelve metre single-crew aircraft, pale warm grey hull, one circular nose intake that always leads.

STYLE — 1985 television documentary photography shot on 16mm, low raking sun, red rock and violet shadow.

Shot one, three seconds. Inside a wide red rock canyon at low sun, <Subject 1> follows the gorge below the rim.
Shot two, two seconds. Tighter on <Subject 1>, warm reflected light sliding up the pale grey hull.

AUDIO — diegetic only: a hard flat thruster drone, turbulent air off the rock.
